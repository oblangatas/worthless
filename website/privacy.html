Worthless Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · 2026-11-26

This Policy explains what personal data is processed when you use the Worthless open-source software, visit the wless.io website, or contact the project. Worthless is run by the Operator: the maintainer known as oblangatas, based in Israel. Contact: privacy@wless.io.

1. The short version

  • The Worthless software runs on your machine. It sends nothing to the Operator: no API keys, no prompts, no responses, no telemetry.
  • The wless.io website collects nothing. It has no forms, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • Like every website, wless.io is delivered by a hosting provider (GitHub) that sees your IP address. The Operator does not receive those logs.
  • If you email the project, the Operator receives your email.

2. The software

The worthless CLI and its local proxy run on your machine and talk directly to the AI providers whose keys you configure. The Operator runs no server in that path and receives no data from the software. If a future version ever offers usage metrics, they will be off by default, opt-in only, and announced in this Policy and the release notes before they ship.

3. The website

When you open a wless.io page, your browser contacts:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., including its content-delivery network), which hosts the site and receives your IP address, browser details, the page requested and the time. This is governed by GitHub's privacy statement.
  • GitHub (gist.githubusercontent.com), which serves the news feed on the homepage and receives the same request details.

The site's fonts and images are served from wless.io itself. The site loads no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and sets no cookies.

The installer at worthless.sh is served by Cloudflare. When you download it, Cloudflare receives your IP address and request details and may set a short-lived security cookie (such as __cf_bm) on that domain, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Links to other sites (GitHub, Ko-fi, documentation, news sources) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

4. Email you send

Mail to legal@wless.io, privacy@wless.io and security@wless.io is received through Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to the Operator's personal mailbox. The Operator uses what you send only to read and answer it, and to handle security reports (legal basis where GDPR applies: legitimate interests). Correspondence is deleted within 12 months of the last message, or sooner if you ask, unless the law requires keeping it.

5. The GitHub repository

Issues, pull requests and discussions at github.com/oblangatas/worthless are hosted by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy statement. Anything you post there is public.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to its use. Send requests to privacy@wless.io; the Operator answers within one month. You may also complain to your data-protection authority (in the EU, your national authority; in the UK, the ICO; in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority).

7. Transfers

GitHub and Cloudflare are US-based providers that offer standard contractual clauses and EU-US Data Privacy Framework certification for international transfers. The Operator is in Israel, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection.

8. Children

The site and software are meant for software developers. The site collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

9. Changes

If this Policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date before any new practice starts. If the project ever starts collecting personal data (for example, a sign-up form), this Policy is updated first.

10. Governing law

This Policy is governed by the laws of the State of Israel. Nothing in it limits rights you have under the mandatory law of the country where you live, or your right to complain to your data-protection authority.

11. Contact

  • General: legal@wless.io
  • Security: security@wless.io
